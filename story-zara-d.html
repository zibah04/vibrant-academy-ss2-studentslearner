Zara D.

The Storyteller

MEET THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAMESS2 / 04
✳ ABOUT MEVIBRANT ACADEMY · SS2

I have always liked the way a story can take a familiar day and reveal something extraordinary inside it. Reading and writing help me notice people, places, and moments that I might otherwise overlook.

Books give me new perspectives, and writing gives me a chance to share my own. I enjoy choosing the right words, asking thoughtful questions, and listening to the different ways people describe the same experience.

When I think of our SS2 class, I do not only think of lessons and assignments. I think of conversations with friends, moments of teamwork, and the small memories that become funny stories later. Every person in our class adds a different chapter.

I would like to keep growing as a reader, a writer, and a person who listens carefully. There are still so many stories to discover and so many ideas worth sharing. Perhaps one day, looking back at this website will remind us how our own stories began.

“Every good story starts with a little curiosity.”
