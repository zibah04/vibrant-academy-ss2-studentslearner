MEET THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAMESS2 / 03
✳ ABOUT MEVIBRANT ACADEMY · SS2

Whenever I see a website, a game, or a new gadget, I start wondering what is happening behind the scenes. I like discovering the small steps that turn an idea into something that actually works.

Coding and games are exciting to me because they combine imagination with problem-solving. I enjoy figuring out patterns, testing different approaches, and learning from the mistakes that happen along the way.

One thing I appreciate about SS2 is that we are not all interested in the same subjects. We can teach one another useful things, from a shortcut on the computer to an easier way to understand a difficult topic. Working together makes each challenge feel smaller.

My goal is to keep improving my skills and create projects that people can genuinely use. I know there is a lot left to learn, but that is part of what makes technology interesting. Every new skill feels like another door opening.

“Curious about how things work and how to build them.”
