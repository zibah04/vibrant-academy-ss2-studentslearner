The Dream Chaser

MEET THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAMESS2 / 06
✳ ABOUT MEVIBRANT ACADEMY · SS2

I enjoy discovering new interests and imagining the possibilities they might lead to. A new song, a fresh idea, or a conversation with a classmate can sometimes inspire me to think about the world differently.

Music helps me relax and be creative, while school gives me opportunities to explore skills I have not tried before. I believe that being open to learning is one of the best ways to find out what you are capable of.

Our class is a mixture of personalities, talents, and ambitions. We are still figuring out many things, but we are doing it together. I like that everyone brings something different to the room, even on ordinary days.

In the coming years, I hope to keep learning, growing, and taking chances on the things that matter to me. I do not need to have every answer right now. I just want to make the most of this part of my journey and encourage others to chase their own dreams.

“Learning something new is my favorite kind of adventure.”
