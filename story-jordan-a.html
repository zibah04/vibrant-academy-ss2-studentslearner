Jordan A.

The Big Thinker

MEET THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAMESS2 / 01
✳ ABOUT MEVIBRANT ACADEMY · SS2

I am the sort of person who likes to ask one more question, even when everyone else thinks the conversation is finished. I enjoy finding out why things work, and I believe that curiosity can turn an ordinary school day into a discovery.

Science and puzzles are especially interesting to me because there is usually more than one way to approach a problem. I like listening to other people's ideas before settling on my own answer. Sometimes a classmate will notice something that I missed entirely.

Our SS2 class is filled with different personalities, and that is what makes it special. We learn together, encourage one another, and occasionally have a good laugh when an experiment or an idea does not go according to plan.

When I think about the future, I hope I will keep the confidence to explore unfamiliar things. I want to use what I learn to solve practical problems and make a positive difference. For now, I am enjoying this chapter of the story, one interesting question at a time.

“Always asking questions and looking for new ideas.”
